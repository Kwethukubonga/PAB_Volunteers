Download the app

Join activities, keep track of your hours and stay up to date with the team. For Android phones and tablets.

Download for Android

Android 8.0 or later

What you can do

  • Browse volunteer opportunities and join in one tap
  • See your schedule and the hours you have given
  • Read announcements from the Philisa team
  • Available in English, isiXhosa and Afrikaans
  • Keeps working when your signal drops

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android above.
  2. Your phone may warn you about installing from outside the Play Store. Choose Settings and allow it for your browser.
  3. Open the downloaded file and tap Install.
  4. Open the app and sign in with Google or your email address.
Why the warning? The app is not on the Play Store yet, so Android asks you to confirm before installing it. This is normal and the app is safe.
